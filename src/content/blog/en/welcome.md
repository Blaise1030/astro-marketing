Orso brings the everyday work of maintaining an Astro site into one workspace.

For content in configured Astro collections, you can edit posts and help articles in a CMS. Orso writes those changes to the Markdown files in your project, so your content stays with the site.

When a change reaches beyond an article, ask a coding agent to update the site's copy, layout, design, or code. The local preview lets you see the result as you work. For a site connected to GitHub, you can share a preview and publish through the repository's workflow.
