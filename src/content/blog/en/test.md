An Astro content collection gives each entry a schema and a file in your project. With the Orso Astro integration, Orso can show supported collections in its Content tab.

Open an entry to edit its body and structured fields. When you save, Orso updates the source file. Your site preview reflects the change, and the content remains part of the project you keep in GitHub.

The CMS needs the Orso Astro integration and supported collection loaders. For changes outside a collection, such as editing a landing-page component, you can ask the agent.
