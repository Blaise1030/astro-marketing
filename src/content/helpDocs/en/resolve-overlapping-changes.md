## Why it happens

You work on a **change** while teammates publish theirs. **Get latest** brings their published edits into your change. Most of the time the two fit together on their own. When you both edited the same lines — the same headline, say — Orso needs to know which version to keep.

## Let the assistant combine them

Orso first asks the assistant to combine both versions. It keeps what each of you meant where it can, then checks that the site still builds. If it works, your change carries on with both edits, and you can check the result in **Preview**.

## Choose yourself

If the assistant can't settle it, or you'd rather decide, choose **Pick the changes myself**. Orso shows each part that needs a decision by name — "Hero section", "Blog · Launch week" — not as files.

For each part, look at the two previews side by side and choose:

- **Keep your version**
- **Use the live site's version**
- **Keep both** — your text first, then the live site's (only for text that still exists on both sides)

The orange outline marks the part of the page that changed. Switch between desktop and mobile, or type another page address to check it there. **Show code** reveals the underlying text if you want it.

When every part is decided, check **After you save** and choose **Save and continue**.

## Change your mind

Choose **Cancel**, then **Yes, stop**. Your change stays exactly as it was, and you can **Get latest** again later.
