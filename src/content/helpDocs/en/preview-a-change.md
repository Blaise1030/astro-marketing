## Open the preview

Save an edit and the **Preview** pane reloads on its own. Orso runs the preview on your computer, so you can check layout, copy and links before sharing anything. Switch between desktop and mobile widths from the toolbar.

## Open an entry's page

In **Content**, choose **Open preview** on a post or article to open its page in a new browser tab, so you keep your place in the editor.

## If the preview looks stale

Refresh the preview from the toolbar. If it has crashed, restart it and the page comes back. When you ask the assistant for a change, it checks the preview itself and restarts it if needed.
