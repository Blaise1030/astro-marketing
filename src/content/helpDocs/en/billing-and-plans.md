## Orso is free

There is no Orso subscription, seat count, or usage meter. Download the app, open your site, and edit.

## What you might still pay for

Hosting (for example GitHub Pages or Cloudflare), your coding agent, and any paid GitHub plan stay with those providers. Orso does not bill you for them.

## Self-host and export

Your content stays Markdown in a repository you control. If you leave Orso, the site still builds and deploys the same way.
