## When to share a preview

Use a preview link when a **change** is ready for other people to look at, but not ready to go **live**. Reviewers see the site as it stands in that change — visitors on the live site are unaffected.

Share early for copy and layout feedback. Share again after bigger edits so everyone is looking at the same version.

## Create a shareable link

1. Open the **change** you want reviewed.
2. Choose **Share preview**.
3. Copy the link and send it to your reviewers.

Anyone with the link can open the preview. They do not need to publish anything, and the link never makes the change live on its own.

## What reviewers see

The preview shows the site with this change applied: pages, copy, images, and navigation as they currently stand. It updates as you keep editing — the same link points at the latest version of the change, so you usually do not need a new link after each tweak.

If something looks out of date, ask the reviewer to refresh the page. For your own local preview pane, see [Preview a change before you publish](/help/preview-a-change/).

## Collect feedback

Ask reviewers to check the pages that changed and leave comments (in chat, email, or wherever your team already reviews work). Typical things to call out:

- Does the copy read clearly?
- Do links and buttons go where they should?
- Does the layout hold up on a phone-sized screen?

Update the change from their notes, then send them back to the same preview link. Repeat until the change looks right.

## After the preview looks good

When reviewers are happy, ask for a **greenlight** on the change. Once it is approved, choose **Publish** to send it live. [Publish your site](/help/publish-your-site/) covers greenlight and publish in more detail. For inviting people to the project itself, see [Work with your team](/help/work-with-your-team/).
