## Create a shareable link

Choose **Share preview** to get a link to your **change**. Anyone with the link can view the site as it stands — nothing goes live yet.

## Collect feedback

Ask reviewers to check the preview and leave comments. Update the change and the same link shows the latest version. When it looks right, ask for a **greenlight**, then **Publish**.
