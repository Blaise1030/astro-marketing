## First steps

Explore the site, read our guides, and start shaping your product story.

1. Review your site settings.
2. Update your home page sections.
3. Share your work with your team.

Each step takes only a few minutes, and you can come back to any of them later.

## More first steps

Once the basics are in place, make the site your own:

- **Tell your story in the hero.** Lead with the one outcome your customers care about most, and keep the headline short.
- **Show what you offer.** List your key features, and add pricing so visitors know what to expect.
- **Build trust.** Add customer testimonials and the logos of teams that already use your product.
- **Answer common questions.** Fill in the FAQ with the questions you hear most often from prospects.
- **Publish a first blog post.** Announce your launch or share what you're working on next.

## Customize your look

Make the site feel like your brand:

- Add your logo and pick colors that match your product.
- Check how every page looks in both light and dark mode.
- Preview the site on a phone as well as a laptop — many visitors will arrive on mobile.

## Work with your team

Great sites are rarely built alone.

1. Invite teammates so they can suggest and review changes.
2. Agree on who owns each page, such as pricing or the blog.
3. Review changes together before they go live.

## Publish your site

When you're happy with your changes, publish them so everyone can see them. Every published change replaces the live version of your site, so take a final look at your pages before you publish.

After publishing:

- Visit your live site and click through each page.
- Share the link with your team and a few friendly customers for feedback.
- Keep improving — small, regular updates beat one big rewrite.

## Tips for success

- **Write for your customer.** Talk about the problems you solve, not just the features you've built.
- **Keep it short.** Clear, simple sentences are easier to read and remember.
- **Use one main call to action.** Decide the single thing you want visitors to do, and make it easy to find.
- **Revisit often.** Update your pages whenever your product, pricing, or customers change.

## Next steps
